# Sample answers Test Speaking IELTS cho Band 6.0

## Part 1: Band 6.0

**Q: Do you use technology every day?**
Yes, I use technology every day, especially my smartphone. I check messages, social media, and also study English with it.

**Q: Which technology do you use the most?**
I use my phone the most because it's very convenient. I also use laptop for school work, but not as much as my phone.

**Q: Do you think your life would be very different without technology?**
Yes, it will be very different. Without technology, life is more boring and more difficult, because I cannot connect with people easily.

**Q: Is there any technology you want to buy?**
I want to buy a new laptop because my old one is very slow. I think a better laptop can help me study and work more effectively.

---

## Part 2: Band 6.0

**Describe a piece of technology you use often.**

I would like to talk about my smartphone. I bought it two years ago. I use it every day for many things.

First, I use it for communication. I can call my parents or chat with friends anytime. Second, I use it to study English. I watch videos, listen to podcasts, and sometimes read articles. Also, it is very useful when I travel because I can use Google Maps to find the way.

I think my phone is very important because without it, my life will be more inconvenient.

---

## Part 3: Band 6.0

**Q: How has technology made working easier?**
Technology makes working easier. Many people use computers and the internet to work from home. It saves time and helps people to be more productive.

**Q: What are the disadvantages of technology in the workplace?**
One disadvantage is that people use technology too much, so they don't spend time with family. Also, sitting too long with a computer can be bad for health.

**Q: Do you think young people adapt to technology more easily than older people?**
Yes, I think so. Young people grow up with technology, so it's natural for them. Older people sometimes don't understand new devices, so they need more time to learn.

**Q: How do you think technology will change in the future?**
In the future, I think technology will be more advanced. Maybe robots will help with housework, and phones will be even smarter. Life will be more convenient, but maybe people will be too dependent on it.
